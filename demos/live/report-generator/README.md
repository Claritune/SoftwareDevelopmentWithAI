# SaaS Metrics Report Generator

A proof of concept that gives a subscription business a one-page view of its health: revenue, customers, churn and engagement, delivered as a single HTML report that opens in any browser.

## What it does

- Holds sample data for customers, subscriptions, invoices and product activity
- Produces an analytics report on demand, with no manual steps
- The report is one self-contained file that can be shared or archived

## What the report shows

- Headline KPIs: total MRR, customer count, churn rate, average revenue per customer
- MRR trend over time
- Revenue by plan tier
- Monthly churn
- Top 10 customers by MRR
- Engagement by activity type

The full specification, including the data model, is in [task.md](task.md).
